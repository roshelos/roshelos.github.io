This policy explains what information Tintara: Tower Defense ("the game", "we") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The game is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

1. Who we are

The game is published by Roshelos gaming studio. Contact us about privacy at tintara.support@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, contact list, photos or location. The game collects only the following.

InformationWhyWho handles it
Gameplay analytics: events such as level started, won, lost or quit, the wave reached, store views and purchases, Rewarded Bonus offers and views, tutorial steps; a random app-instance ID; device model, operating system version, language, approximate region (from IP address, which is not stored by us) and app version. To understand where players get stuck, fix balance problems and measure whether the game is fun. Google Firebase Analytics

Remote configuration: the app-instance ID and app version, to deliver tuning values. To adjust game balance without an update. Google Firebase Remote Config
Advertising data (only when you choose to watch a Rewarded Bonus ad): your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and ad interactions (requested, shown, completed). To show you a rewarded ad, pay for it, measure it and prevent fraud. AppLovin MAX and the ad networks it mediates between
Google Play Games data: if you sign in, your Play Games player ID and display name, your cloud save and your achievements. To keep your progress when you change phone and to unlock achievements. Google Play Games services
Game account (only after Play Games sign-in): a Firebase user ID linked to your Play Games account, and a small profile: the app version, platform (Android or iOS), the game's language, and when the account was first and last used. No name and no email. Not written while "Share usage data" is off. To give each signed-in player one account that later features (for example moving progress between phones) can use. Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore
Weekly leaderboards (Endless mode): a random player ID created on first start, your Endless scores, and your profile name and Avatar, which are shown next to your score. To rank Endless runs each week, show the boards and award the week-end Crystals. Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards)
Purchase information: what you bought, a purchase receipt or token and the time. We never see your payment card or payment details. To give you what you bought, restore it, and prevent fraud. Google Play Billing (through Unity IAP)
Game progress stored on your device: stars, Crystals, upgrades, settings and your profile name. It is encrypted on the device. To save and resume your game. Stays on your device, plus Google Play Games cloud save if you sign in

Your profile name (3 to 16 characters, a generated name such as "Amber Driller 482" unless you change it) is stored with your save. Other players see it, with your Avatar, on the weekly leaderboards when you post an Endless score. Please do not put personal information in it.

3. Advertising and consent

The game shows only rewarded ads, and only when you choose to watch one (for example to double the Crystals from a win, to get a Second Chance, or to double a Supply Drop). There are no interstitial or banner ads. Owning the Supporter Pass gives you every Rewarded Bonus without watching an ad.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent prompt (the User Messaging Platform) before any advertising data is used, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy and consent. You can also limit ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads) and reset or delete your advertising ID there.

4. How we use information

  • To run the game, save your progress and deliver purchases and rewards.
  • To fix bugs and crashes and to improve balance and design.
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch and to pay for them.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to comply with the law.

We do not use your information to profile you for any purpose beyond these, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. We do not sell personal data

We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising networks used for rewarded ads receive the advertising data described above in order to serve and measure the ad; where laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act treat that as "sharing" for advertising, you can opt out through Settings > Privacy and consent or your device's advertising settings.

6. Who else receives information

Only the service providers named above (Google for Firebase, Play Games and Google Play Billing; AppLovin and its ad network partners for rewarded ads; Unity for the in-app purchase library and the weekly leaderboards), who process it for us under their own terms, and authorities where the law requires it. Their policies are at policies.google.com/privacy, applovin.com/privacy and unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required they rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

8. How long we keep it

Firebase Analytics event data is kept for 14 months (the setting we use) and Crashlytics data for 90 days. Advertising data is kept by the ad providers under their own policies. Your Play Games cloud save is kept until you delete it. Your game account profile is kept until you ask us to delete it. Leaderboard scores are kept for the week's board and its results. 9. Your choices and rights

  • Consent and ads: change your choice in Settings > Privacy and consent.
  • Analytics: you can turn analytics collection off in Settings > Privacy and consent.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection: depending on where you live (for example the EU and UK GDPR, California, Canada, Australia and the Philippines) you may have the right to see, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict some processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

10. Deleting your data

To delete the data we hold about you:

  1. Email tintara.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include the app-instance ID shown in Settings > Privacy and consent (so we can find your analytics and crash records) and, if you use it, your Google Play Games player name. We will delete the analytics, crash and configuration records linked to that ID, your game account and its profile, and your leaderboard entries, and confirm by email.
  2. Cloud save and achievements: delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account and data).
  3. On your device: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android Settings.
  4. Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play and, where we must keep them for accounting or legal reasons, for as long as the law requires.

11. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Game saves on your device are encrypted and signed to detect tampering, and our providers protect data in transit. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the game or the store listing about important changes.
