Towers that run on Lumen

A 3D tower defense game for Android. Jelly-and-glass aliens called Siphons march on your Main Building, and every tower runs on Lumen, the glowing liquid in one shared glass tank. Watch the tank drain.

How it plays

How it plays

  1. Siphons march on your Main Building

    They are translucent aliens that crave raw Lumen, and they walk the path straight toward the Main Building.

  2. One tank feeds every tower

    The Main Building holds the Lumen and converts it into the energy each gun fires. Nothing fires without it.

  3. Mix energies

    Each tower has its own pipe from the Main Building and carries one energy. Mix energies to burn, freeze and shock the Siphons.

  4. 4

    Call the drone

    Every shot drains the tank. When it runs low, call the drone to pour in more Lumen. The tank beside this page does the same as you read.
