Towers that run on Lumen

Tintara: Tower Defense is a 3D tower defense game for Android. Jelly-and-glass aliens called Siphons march on your Main Building, and every tower draws Lumen from one shared glass tank and fires its own energy. Watch the tank drain, call the drone to refill it, and mix energies to burn, freeze and shock the Siphons.
