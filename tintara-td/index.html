What Tintara is

Every world glows with Lumen, the living light that runs beneath it. Vorax, a giant cosmic creature with no light of its own, is always thirsty for it. Through its portals Vorax drinks the worlds dim, and the creatures near the portals catch Lumen fever. They crave the glow and march toward the brightest Lumen they can find. These are the Lumivores.

You are one of the Keepers, inventors from Tintara, a world Vorax has already drained dim. You land a Main Building on each world, bring Lumen home from a spring with your drone, turn it into seven kinds of energy and fire it from towers. A hit breaks the fever and the Lumivore pops, cured. Beat a world's boss and its portals close, and the world glows again.

The road Lumen takes

  1. Spring. Lumen wells up at the edge of the map.
  2. Drone. It flies there and carries Lumen home.
  3. Tank. It pours into the glass tank on the Main Building. An empty tank means silent towers.
  4. Chambers. Seven glass chambers shape raw Lumen into seven energies: Burn, Shock, Freeze, Splash, Pierce, Acid and Pull.
  5. Pipes. A glowing pipe runs from the chambers to every tower.
  6. Towers. A tower gathers one shot of energy and fires it.
  7. Lumivores. A hit breaks the fever, and the Lumivore pops, cured.

How it plays

  1. Lumivores march on your Main Building

    They crave raw Lumen, and they walk the path straight toward the Main Building.

  2. One tank feeds every tower

    The Main Building holds the Lumen and converts it into the energy each tower fires. Nothing fires without it.

  3. Mix energies

    Each tower has its own pipe from the Main Building and fires one energy. Mix energies to burn, freeze and shock the Lumivores.

  4. 4

    Call the drone

    Every shot drains the tank. When it runs low, call the drone to pour in more Lumen. The tank beside this page does the same as you read.

Eleven worlds

Ten levels in each world, each with its own ground, sky, Lumivores, World Hazard and boss. A twelfth world, the Pantry, where Vorax keeps everything it drank, is still to come.
